Refund Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

Current status: Cook Better Co. is not currently charging for a subscription or paid digital service in this prototype. No “one-month free trial” is currently offered from this page.

1. No current paid subscription

Because the current prototype does not process subscription payments, there are currently no subscription charges to refund through Cook Better Co.

2. Future paid subscriptions

Before accepting payment, Cook Better Co. will publish the actual subscription price, billing interval, trial terms (if any), renewal terms, cancellation process, and refund rules. Customers will receive the material billing terms before being charged.

3. Grocery purchases

Cook Better Co. does not currently sell or ship groceries. Grocery purchases made through Kroger or another third-party retailer are governed by that retailer's checkout, cancellation, substitution, delivery, and refund policies.

4. Duplicate or erroneous charges

If a future Cook Better Co. billing system produces a duplicate or unauthorized charge, the published support channel should be used promptly so the transaction can be investigated and corrected where appropriate.

5. Legal rights

This policy does not limit non-waivable consumer rights under applicable law.
