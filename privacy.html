Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

Launch checklist: Before this policy is used for a paid/public launch, replace the business-detail placeholders with the actual legal business/entity name, support email, and any required mailing address or privacy-contact details.

This Privacy Policy explains how Cook Better Co. handles information when you use this website and its grocery-planning features. It is written to match the current prototype and should be updated whenever the site starts collecting new information, adds analytics, advertising, payments, customer accounts, or other integrations.

1. Information we currently handle

  • Local profile information: If you choose to use the current local-profile prototype, your name and optional email are stored in your browser's local storage. They are not sent to our server by this prototype.
  • Kroger connection information: When you connect Kroger, Kroger's OAuth process authorizes access. Our server stores encrypted OAuth access/refresh tokens in a secure, HttpOnly session cookie so the requested cart operation can work. We do not put the Kroger client secret in the browser.
  • Grocery-search information: A ZIP code, selected store/location ID, recipe ingredient search terms, and selected product information are sent to our server so it can request store/product data from Kroger. We do not intentionally save these searches in a customer database in the current prototype.
  • Technical information: Our hosting/API infrastructure may receive normal technical request information such as IP address, browser/device information, timestamps, and error logs. Retention and processing by infrastructure providers is governed by their applicable terms and privacy documentation.

2. Why we use information

  • To provide recipe and grocery-planning functionality.
  • To locate Kroger stores and retrieve Kroger product information.
  • To connect an authorized Kroger account and add selected products to the customer's Kroger cart.
  • To maintain security, prevent abuse, troubleshoot errors, and keep the service functioning.
  • To respond to support or privacy requests when a real support channel is published.

3. What we do not currently use

The current prototype does not intentionally run Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, advertising trackers, behavioral advertising, or embedded third-party video/social widgets. Do not add those services without updating this policy and the cookie/consent approach first.

4. Third parties

Kroger: Kroger receives requests needed to provide its store, product, and cart APIs and handles OAuth authorization. Your use of Kroger services is also subject to Kroger's own terms and privacy practices.

Unsplash: Recipe and hero imagery currently loads from Unsplash URLs. Your browser therefore makes requests to Unsplash when those images load. Image licensing should be verified for each asset before commercial launch.

Hosting/infrastructure: The site is deployed on Vercel. Infrastructure providers may process technical request data as necessary to operate the service.

5. Data retention

The current local profile remains in the browser until the user or browser clears it. Kroger session cookies expire or are cleared when the session is invalid. Server logs may have their own retention periods. Before launch, set and document specific retention periods for any production database, support system, analytics system, or billing system that is added.

6. Security

We use HTTPS, server-side environment variables for secrets, HttpOnly/Secure cookies for the Kroger session, and encryption for the stored session payload in the current implementation. No security system is perfect, so this is not a guarantee of absolute security.

7. Your privacy choices and rights

Depending on where you live and whether a particular privacy law applies to Cook Better Co., you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of personal information, and in some circumstances opt out of certain processing. Kentucky's Consumer Data Protection Act applies to covered businesses meeting statutory thresholds; it became effective January 1, 2026. If the law applies to us, we will provide the rights and appeal process required by that law.

Privacy requests: Publish a real privacy-contact email before launch. Do not use a placeholder email in production.

8. Children's privacy

The service is not designed as a child-directed service. Do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through a production account system without completing the requirements of the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and any other applicable law.

9. Changes

We may update this policy when the service changes. The date above will be updated when a material revision is published.
